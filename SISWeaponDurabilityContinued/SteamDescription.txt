This is an update to SweetCaptn's mod [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3487805258]SIS Weapon Durability[/url] that fixes a few bugs, adds a couple features and several new configuration options. This mod replaces the original mod, so do not use them together. This was tested on the publicbeta version.

[h2]What is this?[/h2]
This mod adds [b]weapon durability[/b] and repair to the game. Your weapons can now break, adding a whole new dimension to your gameplay!

[b]Recommended to start a new game.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Weapons now have durability and will degrade by usage. Different weapons have different durabilities. All can be tweaked through the settings.
[*] Can affect player community and / or NPCS.
[*] Weapon price and damage affected by their durability.
[*] Repair melee weapons by using Duct Tape or guns with Gun Repair Kits. Can be found in the world or crafted at the workbench.
[*] Visible Durability bar for weapons. Changes color as the durability goes down.
[*] Adds 7 new equipment items: Animal fat, Glue, Homemade Glue, Junk Parts, Gun Parts, Duct Tape and Gun Repair Kit.
[*] Supports any weapons added by mods.
[/list]

[h3] Detailed Information [/h3]

Every weapon in the game now has durability, the speed at which it degrades, and the  amount of parts that are given when it breaks. Each weapon type has its own maximum durability, with some weapons breaking much faster than others (such as planks or frying pans).

Whenever a weapon breaks, there's a chance it will drop parts which can be used at a workbench with Glue to create Duct Tape (will require some construction skill), or Gun Parts which can be combined to make Gun Repair Kits.

Weapons are repaired by using Duct tape or Gun Repair Kits which can also be found around the world or bought from traders. To repair a weapon, just select the "Repair" action in the inventory page for that specific weapon. If you have the necessary items in your inventory, the weapon will be repaired to a certain state. A gun repair kit always fully repairs a gun, but duct tape only repairs a melee weapon partially.

When selling weapons, their durability affects their prices. A very damaged weapon won't be as valuable as another one in pristine condition. Same thing happens with their damage, the less durability they have, the less damage they will do.

[h3]Recipes[/h3]
[list]
[*] Make Homemade Glue. Made at campfire with pot. Requires 2 Animal Fat + 100 Water.
[*] Make Gun Repair Kit. Made at workbench. Requires Construction level 3 + 5 Gun Parts.
[*] Make Duct Tape. Made at workbench. Requires Construction level 3 + 10 Junk Parts + 1 Glue (or Homemade Glue).
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
The settings file can be found inside the mod's folder under "Settings_SISWeaponDurability.xml".
[list]
[*] (New) LoggingEnabled. Enables writing debug messages to log.txt (default false)
[*] (New) EnabledForPlayer. Whether weapons will degrade for you. (default true)
[*] EnabledForPlayerCommunity. Whether weapons will degrade for characters in your community. (default true)
[*] EnabledForNPCs. Whether weapons will degrade for NPCs. (default false)
[*] EnabledForMeleeWeapons. Whether durability is enabled for melee weapons. (default true)
[*] EnabledForAmmoWeapons. Whether durability is enabled for guns. (default true)
[*] (New) SkinningReducesDurability. (default true)
[*] (New) SkinningDurabilityDegradePercentage. Amount of durability lost when skinning. (default 0.05)
[*] (New) ChoppingReducesDurability. Whether axes will degrade when chopping. (default true)
[*] (New) ChoppingDurabilityDegradePercentage. Amount of durability lost when chopping. (default 0.05)
[*] (New) MiningReducesDurability. Whether pickaxes will degrade when mining. (default true)
[*] (New) MiningDurabilityDegradePercentage. Amount of durability lost when mining. (default 0.05)
[*] (New) AssassinationReducesDurability Whether knives will degrade when assassinating an NPC. (default true)
[*] (New) AssassinationDurabilityDegradePercentage Amount of durability lost after assassinating. (default 0.05)
[*] WeaponsSpawnWithLessDurability. Whether weapons spawn with less durability. (default true)
[*] (New) MinWeaponDurabilityOnSpawnPcnt. Minimum durability a weapon will spawn with. (default 0.35)
[*] (New) MaxWeaponDurabilityOnSpawnPcnt. Maximum durability a weapon will spawn with. (default 0.8)
[*] HitLocationAffectsDegradation. Whether hit location (head, top, bottom) affects degradation of weapons. (default false)
[*] (New) HitLocationHeadModifierPcnt. Multiplier for hitting targets in the head. (default 0.9)
[*] (New) HitLocationTopModifierPcnt. Multiplier for hitting targets in the torso/arms. (default 1.1)
[*] (New) HitLocationBottomModifierPcnt. Multiplier for hitting tagets in the legs. (default 1.0)
[*] ShowEquipmentNotificationOnWeaponBroken. Whether to show an equipment notification when a weapon breaks. (default true)
[*] ShowStatusMsgOnWeaponBroken. Whether to show a status message when a weapon breaks. (default false)
[*] CharactersSpeakOnWeaponBroken. Whether a character will say something when their weapon breaks. (default true)
[*] PlaySoundOnWeaponBroken. Whether to play a sound when a weapon breaks. (default true)
[*] (New) GlobalWeaponBaseDamageMultiplier. Multiplier for the base damage (not total damage including skill) of all weapons. (default 1.0)
[*] (New) DeterioratedWeaponMinimumDamagePercent. Deterioration will not reduce a weapon's base damage below this percentage. (default 0.1)
[*] GlobalDurabilityDegradeRate. General durability degrade rate. (default 1.0)
[*] DefaultBowDurability. Max durability for bows not defined in the settings files. (default 300)
[*] DefaultGunDurability. Max durability for guns not defined in the settings files. (default 300)
[*] ChanceAnimalFatOnSkinAnimal. Chance to obtain animal fat when skinning animals. (default 0.75)
[*] (New) ChanceAnimalFatOnSkinHuman. Chance to obtain animal fat when skinning non-zombie humans. (default 0.50)
[*] (New) ChanceAnimalFatOnSkinZombie. Chance to obtain animal fat when skinning zombies. (default 0.25)
[*] (New) DisassemblyTime. Amount of time it takes to disassemble weapons. (default 15.0)
[*] WeaponDurabilityBarColor. The color of the durability bar at maximum durability. (default 255, 221, 0, 255)
[*] WeaponTypes. List with the settings for each weapon.
[*] ExcludedWeapons. List of weapons that will not degrade with use.
[/list]
